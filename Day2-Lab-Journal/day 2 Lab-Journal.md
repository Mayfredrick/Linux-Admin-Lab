\## Lessons Learned



Today I learned how Linux controls access through users, groups, ownership, and permissions.



Instead of assigning permissions to every individual user, administrators can assign users to a group such as "homelab" and manage permissions at the group level.



This simplifies administration and supports the principle of least privilege.
